SVG Viewer for Confluence — Privacy & Data Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

What the app does with data

SVG Viewer for Confluence runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. When a page containing the macro is viewed, the app reads the SVG attachment of that Confluence page and renders it in the viewer. That is the only content the app touches.

What we store

Nothing. The app keeps no database, stores no page content, no attachments, no personal data and no usage history. Macro settings are stored by Confluence itself as macro parameters inside your page.

Where processing happens

All processing happens inside Atlassian's infrastructure (Forge). The app makes no calls to any external service — there is no data egress. It is designed to meet the requirements of Atlassian's Runs on Atlassian program.

Security

SVG files are sanitized before rendering: scripts, foreign objects and non-HTTPS links are removed. Links open in a new tab with rel="noopener noreferrer".
