Questions vendors ask

Can't I just run Atlassian's connect-to-forge codemod myself?

Yes, and for the wrapper that is where we start too. What the codemod does not do: test each module on a dev site, configure the remote and FIT auth, add the migration-intent banner, handle unlicensedAccess, and get the version through Marketplace review. That is the $900 — and it is credited in full if you go Native within 90 days. The native rewrite is not automated by anyone.

Do my customers have to reinstall?

No. A Forge app that declares your Connect key is released as a new version of the same listing. Installs, reviews and licenses carry over; updates roll out automatically in batches over up to 10 days.

What can't be migrated?

Jira issue field values are not retained on migration (fields are re-created; values need a migration script). Confluence macros can't render in the legacy editor. Some web item locations, Jira reports, blueprints and JSM reports have no Forge equivalent yet and get rebuilt as pages. Your map lists every one of these before you pay anything.

My backend has a database. Does it have to move?

Not for the wrap: it stays as a Forge Remote. For native, we move state to Forge Storage or Forge SQL where that makes sense; if a remote must stay, you keep everything else but won't qualify for Runs on Atlassian.

Who am I working with?

An independent engineer (Ukraine/EU) who has spent 2026 automating Connect-to-Forge migrations with Claude Code, plus a small bench for frontend work. You will talk to the person doing the work. References and a sample migration are in our GitHub.

What if Atlassian moves the deadline again?

They already did once (December 2026 → 31 January 2027). The update freeze since March 2026 and the admin banners are live today regardless of the date, and the 25% revenue share starts 1 October 2026.
