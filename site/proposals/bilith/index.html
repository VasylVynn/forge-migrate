Portfolio deal

These apps are four families of near-clones (Google Workspace, Office 365, Asana, Box) sharing one architecture: an iframe embed, OAuth to the external service, and a page/macro module per host product. We migrate the first app of each family at full effort, then stamp the siblings from it. Whole portfolio, all listings: $19,900 fixed, in 4 tranches ordered by installs (Google first), 25% down per tranche, balance on Marketplace approval per tranche. Single-family option: $6,900 (e.g. the four Google Drive & Docs apps first).

Why these apps specifically need the remote decision early

Every Bilith app talks to an external service (Google, Microsoft, Asana, Box) with OAuth flows — the exact class of app where popups and token exchange break naively on Forge. Two viable end-states: keep a thin Forge Remote for OAuth (fastest, no Runs on Atlassian), or move token handling into Forge with the external OAuth provider pattern (slower, badge-eligible). The per-app maps above assume the first; we quote the second on request.
