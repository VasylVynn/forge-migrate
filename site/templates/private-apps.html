Your internal app says LEGACY. Here is what that means and what it costs to fix.

Since 28 May 2026, Atlassian marks every private (custom) Connect app in Apps → Connected apps with a warning and a LEGACY label. On 31 January 2027 Connect reaches end of support: the app keeps running "at your own risk", with no updates or security fixes from Atlassian, and parts of it can stop working without notice.
